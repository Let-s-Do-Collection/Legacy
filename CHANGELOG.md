[1.0.3]

**Changed**
* Baker and Master Baker, as well as Herbalist and Tea Master, are now a progression like Farmer, Master Farmer and Cultivator. The second title stays hidden until you have the first
* "Heart of the Mountains" and "Hero of the Village" now come after your name, so it reads "Name, Heart of the Mountains"
* Legacy now only counts what a title or journey actually needs, so world saves stay much smaller on busy servers
* Every tooltip in the journal now has a frame with an icon next to the name. Titles and milestones are framed in their rarity, everything else in a simple frame
* Unlocked titles, milestones and the server history now show a tooltip when you hover over them
* Legacy now has a config screen, through Mod Menu on Fabric and the mod list on NeoForge. The options tab in the journal still works and changes the same settings. Your old settings are carried over

**Fixed**
* In the creative inventory, the tab page buttons now move with the inventory when the journal is open
* In the creative inventory on Fabric, the page arrows no longer cover the journal button. The button now moves below them
* In the creative inventory, you can now type in the journal search. Pressing T or a number key no longer jumps to the search tab or swaps your hotbar
* The server log no longer fills up with the same warning over and over when a title uses a statistic that does not exist
* Titles with a comma like "Beyond the Horizon" no longer show an extra space before the comma
* The text on the milestone toast is readable again
