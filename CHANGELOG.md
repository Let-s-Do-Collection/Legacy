[1.0.0]

**Initial Release**

Welcome to Legacy! A purely cosmetic mod that remembers what you did. Unlock titles by farming, mining, exploring and fighting, and wear one above your name, its colour showing the rarity. Keep a personal journey of your first times, sorted by theme and dated by world day. And race for server milestones: the first player to reach one is recorded forever in the Server History.

No skills, no levels, no gameplay bonus. Every title, journey and milestone is a data pack file, so you can add your own.
